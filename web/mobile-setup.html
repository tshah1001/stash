📱 Mobile Setup Guide

Save articles from any app on your phone with just 2 taps

What you'll be able to do:
  • Save articles from Safari, Chrome, NYT app, Substack, Twitter, and more
  • Extract full article content (even from paywalled sites you're logged into)
  • Save with 2 taps: Share → Stash
  • Works offline with automatic syncing

Option 1: Web Share Target (Recommended)

Best for quick saves of public articles. Works everywhere on iOS.

Open Stash in Safari
Make sure you're viewing this page in Safari (not Chrome or another browser).
Install as App

Tap the Share button (square with arrow) at the bottom of Safari

Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"

Tap "Add" in the top right

Open Stash from Home Screen
Find the Stash icon on your home screen and tap it once. This registers it as a share target.
Test It!

Open Safari and go to any article (try nytimes.com or any blog)

Tap the Share button

Scroll down to find "Stash" in the list

Tap "Stash" to save the article

✨ That's it! Now you can save from ANY app that has a Share button - NYT, Substack, Twitter, Safari, etc.

Web Share Target Setup

Install Stash as a PWA and it will appear in your share menu.

Open Stash in Chrome
Make sure you're viewing this page in Chrome (the default Android browser).
Install the App

Tap the menu (⋮) in the top right

Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"

Tap "Install" or "Add"

Open Stash from Home Screen
Find the Stash icon and tap it once. This registers it as a share target.
Test It!

Open Chrome and go to any article

Tap the Share button

Look for "Stash" in the share menu

Tap "Stash" to save the article

✅ Done! You can now save from any Android app that has a share function.
Note: Android's Web Share Target works great for most public articles. For paywalled content, you may need to use the bookmarklet method (see below).

Troubleshooting

Stash doesn't appear in share menu

  • Make sure you installed Stash as a PWA (Add to Home Screen)
  • Open the Stash app at least once from your home screen
  • Try restarting your phone
  • Make sure you're sharing a URL/web page (not an image or file)

Article content is missing or incomplete

  • This happens with paywalled sites - use the iOS Shortcut method instead
  • Some sites block content extraction - the article will still save with URL and title
  • You can manually open the article later from Stash

Offline saves not syncing

  • Make sure you have an internet connection
  • Open the Stash app - this triggers sync
  • Check browser console for errors (for developers)

Share button missing on some apps

  • Not all apps support sharing web URLs
  • Try copying the URL and pasting it directly in Stash
  • Or open the link in Safari/Chrome first, then share from there
